About Downunder Games Studio

A small independent game studio in Australia, making games we genuinely want to play.

Who we are

Downunder Games Studio started with a simple idea: take a small, tight gameplay idea and polish it until it's genuinely fun. No big budget, no filler — just a studio in Australia doing careful, player-friendly work.

That's how Pirate Haven came together: a roguelike action game about sailing, ship-to-ship cannon fights, and one more run before bed.

What we care about

  • Tight, replayable gameplay over spectacle
  • Clear, friendly, low-friction experiences
  • Small studio, big attention to detail

Right now

Pirate Haven is live on Steam, and we're watching the leaderboards to see how far players will push the world level.

Coming up

We're already working on our next project. Nothing to announce just yet — but if you follow along, you'll hear about it first.
