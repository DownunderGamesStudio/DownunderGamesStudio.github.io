Released game

Pirate Haven

Sail the seas as a pirate captain in this challenging roguelike action adventure. Battle other pirates, search for booty, and upgrade your ship at ports - every run gets harder, and every rank up gets more interesting.

Action Adventure Indie Single-player

As the world level climbs, more interesting events start to happen: bigger ships, stronger cannons, and more to gain — but the enemy pirates level up too. Score, exp, and upgrades all scale with the challenge, so there's always a run worth pushing.

Buy on Steam Community Hub

What you'll be doing

  • Explore a vast procedurally generated world
  • Fight other pirates in ship-to-ship combat
  • Search for booty and upgrade your ship with extra cannons and size
  • Grab power-ups — speed boosts, accuracy boosts, shot velocity boosts
  • Go fishing and try to catch every fish for achievements
  • Survive dangerous boss events and storm events
  • Manually aim cannon shots — estimate the flight time and lead your target
  • Climb the weekly and all-time score leaderboards
  • Max out the world level and see how far you can push it

At a glance

  • Released February 15, 2024 (Early Access from September 2023)
  • Platform: Windows (64-bit), PC
  • Genres: Action, Adventure, Indie
  • Single-player, with Steam Achievements and leaderboards
  • Designed for mouse & keyboard (partial controller support)

Minimum requirements

  • Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel Core i5-3570 or equivalent
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 or equivalent, DirectX 11
  • Storage: 500 MB available space
